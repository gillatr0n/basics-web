02 / The magic is what you make together

Two worlds.
One shared story.

At the table, it's a conversation. In your imagination, it's an adventure. Your choices connect the two.

A few friends. A question worth asking.

Dungeon Master
“The castle door is locked. What do you do?”
You
“I knock. Does anyone answer?”

“I'll look for a window.”

You describe what your character tries. The Dungeon Master tells you what happens, using rules or dice when needed.
